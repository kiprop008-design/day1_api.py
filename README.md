# Crypto Price Checker

A small tool that shows the **live price of Bitcoin** (and other popular cryptocurrencies) in plain, easy-to-read language.

## What it does

When you run it, it checks the current market and tells you:

- The current price in US dollars
- Whether the price has gone up or down in the last 24 hours, and by how much
- For the extended version: the day's highest and lowest price, the total size of the market, and the coin's ranking

No technical knowledge is needed to read the results.

## Example of what you will see

```
Bitcoin (BTC)
------------------------------
Current price: $67,432.15
24h change:    UP 1.84%
```

The numbers are live, so they change every time you check.

## Versions included

- **Bitcoin only:** a quick, simple view of the Bitcoin price
- **Full summary:** Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana side by side with more detail

## Where the information comes from

All prices come from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/), a trusted and free source of cryptocurrency market data. No account or payment is needed.

## Good to know

- If the internet is down, the tool shows a friendly message instead of crashing.
- CoinGecko limits how often its free service can be used. If you see a "too many requests" message, wait a minute and try again.
